Modern Decking

Composite Deck Construction

You can elevate the aesthetic appeal and long-term appraisal value of your South Bay home with eco-friendly, premium composite decking. San Jose Decks & Patios installs top-tier brands like TimberTech, Azek, and Trex Transcend to ensure your deck delivers decades of barefoot enjoyment without constant staining or sealing.

Composite boards are engineered to prevent splintering, cupping, and termite invasion. Because synthetic treatments resist moisture absorption, composite decks do not rot or gray under relentless Northern California summer UV exposure.

Timeless Natural Warmth

Natural Wood Decks: Redwood & Cedar

A natural wooden deck embodies the classic California dream. Real heart redwood and Western red cedar impart organic warmth, natural aromatic resistance to insects, and a comfortable tactile feel that synthetic materials cannot fully replicate.

We select dense, high-grade heartwood that substantially increases your property appraisal value while transforming your backyard into an outdoor haven. Plus, every new wood deck installation includes our special offer of up to $1,000 off commercial UV-protective sealer to preserve color depth and extend structural longevity.

Weather Protection

Custom Patio Covers & Roofs

A covered patio transforms your backyard into an outdoor family room usable in all four seasons. Whether shielding from scorching summer heat or providing a dry haven during gentle winter rain, our patio covers are custom-engineered to match the roofline and architectural aesthetics of your residence.

Styles Built By Our San Jose Crew:

  • Open Gable Roofs: Vaulted high ceilings that maximize airflow and introduce natural light while maintaining complete overhead rain protection.
  • Tongue & Groove Wood Drop Ceilings: Finished underside crafted from finished cedar with integrated dimmable LED downlights, ceiling fans, and outdoor speaker wiring.
  • Shed & Hip Ridge Structures: Built to tie seamlessly into existing home fascias and load-bearing studs with zero water pooling risk.

Architectural Accent

Pergolas & Bioclimatic Louvers

Pergolas add sculptural elegance to garden pathways, poolside lounges, and dining terraces. Featuring heavy timber vertical posts, open crossbeams, and optional privacy lattice, they form filtered shade zones that capture the best of Californian outdoor living.

For high-tech modern properties in Silicon Valley, we also install bioclimatic motorized louvered pergolas. Rotate louvers to track the sun for optimal afternoon shade, or close them tight to form a 100% waterproof seal with internal gutter drainage during rain.
